TECHNICAL APPROACH & ARCHITECTURE

SIH 2026
End-to-End System Architecture
Frontend & GIS Command: React 18, Vite, TailwindCSS, Leaflet.js (2D/3D Topo & Satellite layers)
Backend & ML Microservices: Python 3.13, FastAPI, Uvicorn, SQLite3, Pydantic Schemas
AI Risk Fusion & Vision: Two-Tier LSI + ARI Hydrological Model, Pillow CV Heuristics, Gemini LLM
Voice & Audio Synthesis: Web Speech Recognition (STT), Speech Synthesis (TTS), Web Audio Siren
Open-Source Architecture • Zero Proprietary Licensing
1. Multi-Source Ingestion Pipeline Automated hourly scraping of IMD weather forecasts, CartoSat DEM terrain elevation, and crowdsourced field photos.
2. Two-Tier Landslide Risk Modeling Evaluates Static Susceptibility ($LSI$) and Dynamic Saturation ($ARI$) using Caine empirical intensity-duration curves.
3. Mountain Safe Routing Engine Dynamically flags blocked mountain arteries (NH-10, NH-29, NH-6) and computes safe bypass routes for SDRF convoys.
4. Resilient Last-Mile Alerting Broadcasts in 8 regional dialects with speech audio readouts for non-literate citizens and 2G GSM SMS backup.
